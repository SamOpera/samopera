I build **secure financial products**: Solidity and Rust smart contracts, trading bots and the web apps people use every day.

I studied **Economics** at the University of Lagos, then **Computer Science** at the University of the People, and I'm completing a **Master of Information Technology** at the University of Lagos. Economics shapes how I build: I start from how people actually save, insure, trade and prove what they own, then design the system around that behaviour.

- 🤖 Lead developer of [**4TOOL**](https://4tool.ai), an AI trading agent for Solana used by **1,500+ people**
- 🏛️ Founder of [**TechTribeDAO**](https://x.com/TechTribeDao), a community for Web3 developers
- 📚 Master's thesis: a lending protocol that scores borrowers on their on-chain savings history
- 💼 Open to **software and blockchain engineering roles**, remote or on-site. Reach me at **dev@samsonolakeye.com**
